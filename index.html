My dearest Myriam,

I love you more than words could ever truly express.

I always enjoyed every moment I spent with you at school. Even the smallest conversations with you made my day brighter. I used to look forward to seeing you, and without even realizing it, I’d smile whenever you were around.

I’ll never forget our first hug, our first phone call, and all the little moments we shared together. They may have seemed ordinary to everyone else, but to me, they became some of the most beautiful memories of my life.

Your kindness, your smile, and even your shyness made you so special to me. Being with you made everything feel lighter, happier, and more meaningful. You brought a kind of peace into my life that I had never felt before.

Thank you for every smile, every laugh, every conversation, and every memory we created together. No matter where life takes us, those moments will always have a special place in my heart.

I truly hope you always find happiness, because seeing you happy has always meant so much to me.

You’ll always be someone I’ll remember with warmth, gratitude, and love.
